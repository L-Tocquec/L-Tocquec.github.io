About me

I am currently a third-year PhD student in applied mathematics at Université Paris-Saclay , affiliated with the Laboratoire de Mathématiques d'Orsay .

I am also a member of the Inria teams Mokaplan (Inria Paris) and ParMa (Inria Saclay).

01

Research interests

My thesis focuses on Entropic Optimal Transport and its generalizations. It is supervised by Luca Nenna (Université Paris-Saclay) and Paul Pegon (Université Paris-Dauphine).

More broadly, I am interested in optimal transport, calculus of variations, and optimization.
