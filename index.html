Welcome on my website !

I am currently a third-year doctoral student at the University of Paris-Saclay, affiliated with the Orsay Mathematics Laboratory. I am also a member of the Inria teams MoKaplan (Inria Paris) and ParMa (Inria Saclay).

Research interests

My thesis focuses on Entropic Optimal Transport and its generalizations and is supervised by Luca Nenna (Univ. Paris-Saclay) and Paul Pegon (Univ. Paris-Dauphine). I am particularly interested in optimal transport, calculus of variations and optimization more generally.
