Research

Publications & academic activities

My main research focus is optimal transport, and more specifically its entropic regularization. I am interested in the asymptotic behavior of this regularization as the parameter vanishes: convergence rates, asymptotic expansions, and the relationship with the unregularized problem. More recently, I have also been exploring the geometry of discrete optimal transport, in particular that of its geodesics.
